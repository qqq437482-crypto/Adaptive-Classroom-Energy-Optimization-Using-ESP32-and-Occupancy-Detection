# Adaptive Classroom Energy Optimization

## Research Question

To what extent can occupancy-based and environmental-aware control reduce classroom energy consumption while maintaining comfortable classroom conditions compared to traditional manual control?

---

## Hypothesis

The proposed smart classroom system will reduce energy consumption while maintaining acceptable thermal comfort conditions compared to traditional manual operation.

---

## Problem Statement

Classrooms often waste energy because fans and electrical devices continue operating regardless of the number of students or classroom environmental conditions.

---

## Objectives

1. Measure classroom occupancy using IR sensors.
2. Monitor temperature and humidity using DHT22.
3. Automatically control fan operation.
4. Reduce unnecessary energy consumption.
5. Maintain comfortable classroom conditions.

---

## Hardware

- ESP32
- DHT22
- Dual IR Sensors
- Relay Module
- LCD I2C
- Fan

---

## Variables

### Independent Variables
- Number of students
- Temperature
- Humidity

### Dependent Variables
- Energy consumption
- Occupancy percentage
- Thermal comfort

### Controlled Variables
- Classroom size
- Fan type
- Operating hours

---

## Expected Results

The system is expected to reduce unnecessary fan operation and improve classroom energy efficiency compared to traditional manual control.
